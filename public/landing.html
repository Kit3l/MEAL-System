Our Framework

The Four Pillars of MEAL

Every feature is designed around the four core components of DLCI's monitoring and learning framework.

M
Monitoring
Track indicators, targets, and actuals across all DLCI programmes in real time with traffic-light dashboards.
E
Evaluation
Measure achievement rates, generate donor reports, and assess programme effectiveness across the drylands.
A
Accountability
Full audit logs, role-based access, and transparent data trails for every entry across DLCI programmes.
L
Learning
Capture lessons learned, best practices, and reflections to strengthen future drylands programming.
Features

Everything DLCI needs in one system

From field data collection in remote drylands to donor reporting — all in one secure platform.

Live indicator tracking
Baseline, target, and actual values with automatic achievement rate calculation and traffic-light status across all programmes.
KoboToolbox integration
Field data collected by DLCI teams on mobile phones syncs automatically into the database — even from remote drylands areas.
Portfolio dashboard
See all DLCI projects, achievement rates, overdue milestones, and high risks at a glance across the entire portfolio.
Risk register
Log risks and assumptions with likelihood and impact scoring, mitigation strategies, and responsible person assignment.
Learning module
Document lessons learned, best practices, and programme adaptations — the often-missing L in MEAL, central to DLCI's mission.
Secure & invite-only
Row-level security, invite-only access, full audit logging. Your beneficiary data and programme information stays protected.
How it works

From the field to the dashboard

Data flows automatically from DLCI field teams into a live dashboard leadership can trust.

1
Field collection
DLCI program officers collect data on KoboToolbox — even offline in remote drylands areas.
2
Auto sync
Data syncs to the secure database the moment connectivity is available.
3
Live dashboard
M&E officers see updated indicators, milestones, and risk scores in real time.
4
Donor reports
Generate Excel and PDF progress reports in one click — ready for donors and leadership.
