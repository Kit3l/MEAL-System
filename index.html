Lesson Learned Q1-2025 · WASH Project
Community mobilisation works better through local structures
Engaging village elders and women's groups increased programme uptake by 34% compared to direct field officer outreach. This approach should be replicated in future WASH programming.
📌 Applicable to: WASH, Health, FSL programmes · Entered by: Jane Wanjiku
Best Practice Q4-2024 · Nutrition Programme
Integrating KoboToolbox with real-time dashboards reduced reporting lag by 2 weeks
Configuring webhooks to push data directly to the MEAL database eliminated the manual Excel transfer step, improving data timeliness and accuracy significantly.
